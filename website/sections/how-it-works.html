01 / LESS ADMIN. MORE LIVING.

A shared plan.
Not a spreadsheet.

A weekend away or another grocery run.
Keep the money part simple.

  1. 01

    Get your people together.

    Create a group. Share a private invite.
    No email. No phone number. No signup.

  2. 02

    Paid for something? Add it.

    Enter the amount and who paid.
    Split equally, by amount, percentage, or shares.

  3. 03

    Know where you stand.

    See who owes whom. Settle up your way,
    then record the payment in SplitFree.

You handle the payment. SplitFree handles the record.
No bank connection or money transfers.
